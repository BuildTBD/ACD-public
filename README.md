# Agent Centered Design: public resources

Methods and skills from [Agent Centered Design](https://agentcentereddesign.org), shared openly
so you and your team can bring them into your own work with AI, adapt them, and pass them along.

## About Agent Centered Design

Agent Centered Design (ACD) is a discipline for engineering the conditions for better agent
work. It treats AI agents as the customer of the systems, knowledge and handoffs they work
within.

Its mission, in its founder's words: to enable agents to deliver trusted results that grow
differentiable solutions for the direction and vision the customer is seeking. Continuous
improvement in ACD is measured on more than speed and cost: efficiency, efficacy,
productivity, and continuous transformation.

Learn more at [agentcentereddesign.org](https://agentcentereddesign.org).
